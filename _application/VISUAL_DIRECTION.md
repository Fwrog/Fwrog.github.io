# Measured paths

A visual language drawn from surveying: observations acquire meaning when they share a reference frame. Sparse points, restrained axes and a measured route give the composition its structure. The craft lies in precise relationships rather than ornamental complexity.

Mineral paper, dark blue-green ink and a single surveying-green accent carry the hierarchy. Filled observations represent established work; open forms and directional paths indicate questions that remain ahead. This distinction should stay legible even when the image is viewed at presentation scale.

Scale moves from a small object through a wider spatial field to a chosen next observation. Carefully aligned labels accompany these changes without competing with the geometry. Typography uses a quiet serif title and clear sans-serif annotations, with deliberately generous margins.

The composition is an original schematic, not an experimental result. Every line has a job: registration, connection or direction. Refinement means improving alignment and spacing, removing unnecessary marks and preserving a clear path through the image.
