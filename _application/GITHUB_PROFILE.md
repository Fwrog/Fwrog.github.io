# Hi, I’m Yikai Wu 👋

**From visual perception to reliable spatial understanding.**

M.Sc. student in Urban Informatics and Smart Cities at **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University**. B.Eng. in Remote Sensing Science and Technology from **Southwest Jiaotong University**.

I combine visual, geometric and geospatial information to study perception in real environments. My experience spans tunnel-construction vision, multi-view RGB-D measurement, street-view analysis and quantitative remote sensing.

[Research portfolio](https://fwrog.github.io/) · [中文主页](https://fwrog.github.io/zh/) · [CV](https://fwrog.github.io/files/Yikai_Wu_CV.pdf) · [Email](mailto:yi-kai.wu@connect.polyu.hk) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0004-2501-7877)

### 🔭 Research direction

**Cross-modal learning → reliable spatial perception → active observation.**

My next research question: how can useful depth information during training improve segmentation when only RGB is available at deployment? Longer term, I hope to connect reliable perception with autonomous mapping and embodied intelligence.
