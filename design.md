# Compact academic homepage

Primary audience: desktop readers. Reference: https://wenjing0916.github.io/#about.

Use a compact two-column academic layout: 250px identity/map column and an approximately 800px reading column at ordinary desktop widths. Sticky section navigation and identity column aid scanning. Body 14px, section headings 20px, project titles 15px. Minimal section whitespace; no hero, promotional cards or career-plan section.

About includes background, current work and three research interests. News is a short inset list. Follow CV order after About: Education, Publications (with manuscripts separately collapsed), Research Experience (RGB-D, tunnel, street-view, NeRF, GPP, medical), Projects, Awards. Presentations is omitted because no such record was supplied.

Map uses only Hefei 2003–2021, Chengdu 2021–2025, Hong Kong 2025–2027. HIT is a News event. CV remains a normal link. All content and interaction is bilingual. Narrow screens stack the columns; desktop remains the design priority.

English is the default route; Chinese is an optional translation. Warm ivory background and brown-red links keep the academic sections restrained. All eight research/project entries use compact Why/How/Results rows. The portrait, expandable Hefei school history, verified user-supplied ResearchGate URL and native WeChat QR dialog personalise the sidebar. Service includes the surveying association roles (2021–2023) and two original supplied photos; the award section intentionally lists the 2013 childhood prize only.
